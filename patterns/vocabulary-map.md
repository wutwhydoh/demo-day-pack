# Vocabulary Map — Who's Using Which Words

Category language across the 22, from demos [demo] and websites as of 2026-06-10 [site]. The point isn't who's right — it's that the words are converging faster than the products, and the gaps between what a demo said and what a website says are themselves signal.

## Converging terms

**"Context layer" / "context graph"** — Octave ("THE CONTEXT LAYER — The only brain built for agentic GTM") [site]; Clearskies ("The Context Layer for Revenue AI"; "Connectors solve access. They do not solve context") [site]; Clearskies' customer on stage ("single point of context") [demo]; Syft Data's contextualize framing [demo]; Ruby ("Context is Everything"; "Full Context Sales Support") [site].

**"GTM brain"** — Octave ("Your Agentic GTM Brain"; "always-on GTM brain") [site]; Cedar's customer ("Cedar just acts as my brain for everything") [demo]; and an attendee asked for "the GTM brain for a company" by name in chat [chat].

**"GTM operating system"** — Smoke Signals ("THE GTM OPERATING SYSTEM") [site]; echoed by an attendee asking for "the overarching operating layer for GTM" [chat].

**"Signals"** (used by at least 7 of the 22 — and by the host's own brand) — WhiteWhale ("Custom Sales Signals for Every Account, Every Day") [site]; Smoke Signals + its "Alpha Signals" product [site]; Syft Data, Alfa, Swan ("Signal Based Outbound"), FirstTouch ("Social engagement signals"), Skyp ("Trigger outreach from any signal") [site].

**"Agent / agentic"** — near-universal: Octave ("agentic GTM"), Swan ("agentic workflow… prompt to pipeline"), Deepline ("GTM API Designed for Agents"), Docket ("AI Sales Engineer"), 1mind (named persona agents), Centralize ("Ask Centra") [site].

**"Relationship data / intelligence"** — The Swarm ("The Relationship Intelligence Leader," "Go-To-Network") [site]; Centralize (stakeholder mapping, multithreading) [site].

## Demo-said vs. website-says gaps

- **Syft Data** demoed a Claude MCP workflow on stage [demo]; its website has no MCP or Claude mention [site]. The demoed capability is ahead of the marketing.
- **Ruby**'s demo positioned explicitly against "just the Claude MCP" [demo]; its site never mentions MCP — the contrast argument exists only on stage [site].
- **Smoke Signals** demoed "Signal Beam" [demo]; the homepage brands its signal product "Alpha Signals" and doesn't mention Signal Beam [site].
- **Saber**'s customer compared it head-to-head with Perplexity deep research on stage [demo] — a customer-side comparison the site doesn't yet make [site].
- **Octave** is the alignment counter-example: the demo's "stop giving me signals, give me context" [demo] and the homepage's "Everyone has AI. Context is your edge." [site] are the same sentence in two registers.

## Naming hazards (real, observed live)

Two unrelated companies both pronounced "sift" demoed: **Syft AI** (syftai.com, outbound) and **Syft Data** (syftdata.com, inbound visitor intent) — acknowledged in chat by a vendor [chat]. **Knock** was heard as "knock.io" on stage; its actual domain is knock-ai.com (knock.io is an unrelated infrastructure company) [demo][site]. **Skyp** is Skype-minus-the-e, pronounced "skip" [demo].
