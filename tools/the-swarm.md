**Demo narrative (compressed) [demo]:** The heart of the demo was a two-step Claude + Swarm MCP workflow: (1) upload the account list as CSV into Claude, score against tech-stack and customer-overlap signals via a Salesforce connector → accounts prioritized by intent; (2) "now review this list and use the Swarm MCP to map my entire network" → accounts prioritized by overlapping signals *and* relationships, with per-account intro paths ("we can get in front of this person through this person"). Relationship context filters bad asks (skip the contact who barely overlapped with someone a decade ago; surface shared-alumni paths).
