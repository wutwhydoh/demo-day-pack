# Skyp

**Demo #19 of 22** (stepped in early when FirstTouch hit a Zoom issue) · Presenter (as publicly stated on stage): Scott Desgrosseilliers, founder/CEO of **Wicked Reports** (customer; marketing attribution) [demo] · Domain: **skyp.ai** (verified; "Skype but there's no e," pronounced "skip") [site]

**Positioning as stated [demo]:** AI-personalized cold email: "We fed it a whole big questionnaire of our ICP and why we're different… and then what Skyp does is translate it into personalized outreach, looking at the person's LinkedIn and scraping other data points, but then also taking our offer and wordsmithing it — and then learning as it goes." Prior state: "I was using Instantly, wasn't having success with cold outbound — like dismal open and response rates."

**Website says (as of 2026-06-10) [site]:** Headline: "GTM agents to close the loop." Subhead: "AI-powered outreach for high-value B2B sales. High deliverability built in." Hero self-description: built for B2B teams of 3–20 reps; "No templates. No credits. Every email and LinkedIn message is written from scratch to protect your deliverability… plug your favorite AI agent into our MCP tools." CTA: "Get a Demo." Platform-average stats on page: 96.2% delivery rate, 38.5% open rate, "$2M qualified pipeline from a single campaign" (vendor-claimed averages).

**Category language [demo][site]:** "Cold outbound," "hooks," "wordsmithing," "learns as it goes," "pixel," LinkedIn "autopilot" [demo]; "GTM agents," "One platform, not six tools" [site].

**Claimed proof / metrics [demo]:** "This is 250 people, 97 opened" (~39%, "sometimes maybe better than an email on our warm list"); ~448 LinkedIn connections/followers gained in three months "all on autopilot." (Open rate is an engagement proxy rather than an outcome metric; no reply or meeting numbers were given.)

**Demo narrative (compressed) [demo]:** Showed campaign open rates and the input setup (ICP questionnaire + differentiators + hooks in their own words). Site pixel identifies which email recipients later visited the website. Engagement data recycles into other channels: automated LinkedIn outreach with auto-generated hooks, then exporting clickers as retargeting audiences: "technically it's cold to the platform [but] you've already warmed them up." The presenter also mentioned using "sift" alongside Skyp (which of the event's two Syfts was not specified).

**Pricing model [site]:** Hybrid, public pricing: Teams $149/seller/mo annual (3-seller minimum, self-serve) · Growth $2,499/mo base (demo-gated) · Enterprise custom. No free tier.

**MCP / integration posture [demo][site]:** Not mentioned on stage. On site, explicit and named: "Native MCP server lets Claude — or any MCP-compatible AI agent — create campaigns, add contacts, trigger personalized sends", included in every plan.

**Funding [site]:** Not found.

**Demo vs website:** The demo led with open rates; the site leads with deliverability and MCP-native agent control. The MCP story never came up on stage.
