# Exportly

**Demo #14 of 22** · Presenter (as publicly stated on stage): Meghan Prudencio, GTM engineer at **Webflow** (customer; 100+ reps on the tool) [demo] · Domain: **exportly.ai** (verified) [site]

**Positioning as stated [demo]:** "Exportly is a Chrome extension… it connects our reps to our Clay tables so that Clay tables and the workflows that we build there are just one click away anywhere on the web." Problem: "The best sales plays live in tools like Clay, but our reps are living in LinkedIn, Salesforce, Outreach. So all that power never really reaches them in a form that they're actually using."

**Website says (as of 2026-06-10) [site]:** Headline: "Clay-Powered Prospecting." Subhead: "The most intelligent Chrome Extension for sellers ever built." CTA: "Request Access" (gated: "To manage high demand, we're onboarding users gradually"). Badge: "Official Clay Partner." Pillars: Powered by Clay · Prospect with AI · Find More Contacts ("2x more verified emails and phones").

**Category language [demo][site]:** "Wired to a Clay table behind the scenes" (every sidebar action), "plays," "buying committee enrichment," "contact data waterfalls," "match rates" [demo]; "150+ Data Vendors in One Extension," "Add. Enrich. Convert." [site].

**Claimed proof / metrics [demo]:** "Over 100 reps" at Webflow; "I think we're seeing like 92% match rates compared to some other sources"; "saved a ton of time" (no number).

**Demo narrative (compressed) [demo]:** From LinkedIn Sales Navigator: select a prospect in the Exportly sidebar → "send to CRM" → Clay enriches via waterfalls and adds to Salesforce, with the team controlling exactly which fields flow back to reps. One-click add-to-Outreach-sequence via the same Clay path. From Salesforce/Google: a "buying committee" play targets the right ICP personas, dedupes against existing contacts, and tells reps exactly who to prospect next. Contact-level Salesforce refresh fixes bad phone numbers mid-call. Honest live failure: the live enrichment errored and the presenter recovered by showing previously completed rows.

**Pricing model [site]:** No public pricing; access-gated waitlist motion. Requires a Clay account to power it. It sits on top of customers' Clay spend.

**MCP / integration posture [demo][site]:** Clay (the engine), Salesforce, Outreach, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Chrome [demo]. No MCP mention; AI via Claygent, ChatGPT, "other bleeding edge LLMs" [site].

**Funding [site]:** Not found.
