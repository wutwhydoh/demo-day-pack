# FirstTouch

**Demo #20 of 22** (delayed by a Zoom client update; completed after Skyp) · Presenter (as publicly stated on stage): Adia Toll, head of sales development at **AskElephant** (customer; askelephant.ai — she was its first sales hire, seventh employee) [demo] · Domain: **firsttouch.com** (verified; app at app.firsttouch.ai) [site]

**Positioning as stated [demo]:** Origin story: a founder-led LinkedIn outbound motion run on a $40/month budget produced 50–60 meetings/month — "the problem with it is whenever we tried to scale… everything fell apart. I couldn't see what the SDRs were sending… And that's where FirstTouch really helped me." Philosophy: social-first sequencing — act on people already engaging with your LinkedIn content, qualify them, then move to other channels only when they don't reply.

**Website says (as of 2026-06-10) [site]:** Hero: "Operationalize Social inside HubSpot." Subhead: "FirstTouch enables teams to trigger and track social actions directly inside HubSpot workflows, segments, and reporting dashboards." CTAs: "Get a Demo" / "Add First Touch to Chrome." Framing: "Email and phone have systems. Social is still a series of manual tasks and missing data." Social proof: "150+ revenue teams."

**Category language [demo][site]:** "Signals" (LinkedIn engagement), "qualification agent," "flows," "tasks," "social first sequence" [demo]; "Social Infrastructure for HubSpot," plays like "Inbound Speed-to-Lead" and "The 'Wake Up' Play" [site].

**Claimed proof / metrics [demo]:** "28 opportunities off of this in the last seven days, just off of my account"; dashboard reply rate "98%… I'm through 100%. Apparently I just got another message" (it ticked up live mid-demo). Read these as small-n, engaged-audience numbers — outreach goes only to people already interacting with her content.

**Demo narrative (compressed) [demo]:** Signals → Configure shows everyone who recently engaged with her or the founder's content; engagers go into a flow where a qualification agent checks ICP fit, then auto-drafts measurable tasks (connection requests, LinkedIn messages) to run manually or partially automated. She volunteered the compliance caveat unprompted — careful with LinkedIn ToS, no mass blasting, only already-engaged people. Call/email tasks push to HubSpot and their regular sequencer to build a social-first sequence.

**Pricing model [site]:** Single tier, public: $99/seat/month with 500 credits; self-serve signup; sales touch above 25 seats.

**MCP / integration posture [demo][site]:** LinkedIn + HubSpot + their sequencer [demo]. "FirstTouch MCP toolkit" is a listed plan feature on the pricing page; no Claude mention on the homepage [site].
