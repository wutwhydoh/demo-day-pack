# Avarra

**Demo #12 of 22** · Presenter (as publicly stated on stage): Jon Harper, senior sales leader at **Paychex** (customer; payroll/HR/PEO; reps and leaders across eight states) [demo] · Domain: **avarra.ai** (verified; spelled "Avarra," double r) [site]

**Positioning as stated [demo]:** "AI sales coach": creates custom simulations, reps practice real call scenarios, then receive coaching. Problem: "There's constantly things to do to coach and develop my people, especially my new hires, and I'm still responsible for a number every single week, every single month, every single quarter." It "enables us to truly coach our people while we're out in the field selling business and hitting the number."

**Website says (as of 2026-06-10) [site]:** Headline: "Get reps Conversation-ready in weeks not quarters with AI Training & Role Playing." Subhead: "Scale your best coaches and accelerate rep performance with personalized coaching and AI-driven simulations, at a fraction of traditional enablement costs." CTAs: "Experience Avarra Now" / "Request a Demo." Problem triad: slow ramp, coaching can't scale, best practices locked with top performers.

**Category language [demo][site]:** "Sim" / "custom sims," "Coach Kate" (the named AI coach persona), "run rate" [demo]; "AI Training & Role Playing," ramp "in weeks not quarters" [site].

**Claimed proof / metrics [demo]:** The headline metric of the event: "Our average first year run rate before using Avarra was about $22,000. After using Avarra, up through Q3 of our fiscal year, our average run rate for new hires is about $96,000 ARR." Nine months of use across all 70–80 sales reps. (Caveat: a before/after comparison as stated on stage, with no baseline adjustment described, so read as directional, not controlled.)

**Demo narrative (compressed) [demo]:** The only pre-recorded demo of the day: snippets of a real Paychex associate's 9-minute session with Coach Kate. The associate ran a simulated benefits-discovery call against an AI prospect persona, probing the business impact of losing an engineer to better benefits. Coach Kate then coached, naming what went well (impact follow-up questioning) and what was missed (recruitment challenges, HR technology as an unexplored pain point), and offered to go deeper.
