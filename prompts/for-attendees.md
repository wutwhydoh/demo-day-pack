# Prompt Menu — For Attendees & GTM Operators Evaluating Tools

Load this pack into your AI tool (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor — anything that reads files), then paste a prompt. Replace bracketed parts. If you felt "lost in a Lego shop" during the event — these are for you.

## 1. Stack-fit triage across the 22

> My company: [size, motion (PLG/sales-led), ICP, deal size]. My current stack: [CRM, enrichment, outreach, etc.]. My biggest GTM bottleneck right now: [one sentence]. Read all 22 files in `tools/` plus `patterns/adjacency-map.md` (or start from `tools/INDEX.md` — the one-page cluster/pricing/MCP table — as the fast path). Shortlist the 3–5 tools most worth a demo for *me*, say which cluster each fills, which would overlap with something I already pay for, and which are choose-between pairs where I should only pick one.

## 2. Evaluation-criteria builder (what's table stakes now)

> Read `patterns/macro-patterns.md` and the MCP/integration and pricing fields across `tools/`. Build me an evaluation scorecard for this category: which capabilities are now table stakes across the 22 — derive this from the actual counts in the files rather than assuming — which are genuine differentiators, and which claimed metrics I should discount because they're presenter-stated and uncontrolled. Output as a checklist I can take into vendor calls.

## 3. Category map orientation

> Read `patterns/adjacency-map.md` and `patterns/vocabulary-map.md`. Explain this space to me like I missed the event: what are the 4–5 real clusters (jobs-to-be-done, not marketing categories), why do some vendors say "context" and others "signals" — and does the difference predict anything about what the product actually does — and what's the difference between the tools that sound identical? Keep vendor names attached so I can follow up.

## 4. Get the names right before you email anyone

> Read the name/domain fields in `tools/`. I think I'm interested in [tool name as you heard/noted it]. Confirm the canonical name and domain, and warn me about the known confusions (two different "Syft" companies demoed; several domains differ from what was said on stage).

## 5. Demo-vs-reality check on one tool

> I'm considering [tool]. Read `tools/{slug}.md` end to end, plus its mentions in `patterns/vocabulary-map.md` and `design/craft-notes.md`. Give me: what its customer actually demoed, what its website claims, where the two diverge, its pricing posture, and the three sharpest questions I should ask on a sales call — including one about any claim that was stated without evidence.

## 6. Budget-shaped shortlist

> My tooling budget for this is [$X/month] and I can commit [self-serve only / annual contract / services engagement]. Read the pricing fields across `tools/` (note: all pricing is website-sourced as of 2026-06-10 — none was stated on stage). Which of the 22 can I even start with at my number, and which require a sales process before I'd learn anything?
