> Thank you. Hey, I'm [Meghan] prudencio, and I am a GTM engineer here at webflow. Right now we've been using exportly across over 100 reps. Exportly is a Chrome extension. What it does is connect our reps to our clay tables so that clay tables and the workflows that we build there are just one click away anywhere on the web. The problem that exportly solves is that the best sales plays live in tools like clay, but our reps are living in LinkedIn, Salesforce, Outreach. So all that power never really reaches them in a form that they're actually using. So I'm going to show you how exportly is helping our team with that. Here we are in linkedin sales navigator. Every action in this exportly sidebar — this extension — is wired to a clay table behind the scenes. So I'll click on [a prospect] here — and you can click on multiple people — and just do send to CRM. And what that will do is send him to clay to enrich and then add him to salesforce. So if we go over to clay, what's happening behind the scenes is that exportly has a special column in clay where it will pull in his profile and then it will let clay enrich it. So we'll send that to an enrichment function. It will run contact data waterfalls, look up the account, and we control exactly what data actually flows back to the rep. And every field that comes back is fully customizable. In this case, of course, it failed, probably because of some of the fail states I have in place. But if we look at previous things that went through, we can see that it was able to enrich phone numbers, emails. We can also add people to outreach sequences directly from this flow with one click, where a rep can click on a sequence directly here, click on the sequence they want to send them to, and when they hit send to outreach it will again send them to clay, enrich their information, add them to an outreach sequence — all with one click, which has really saved our reps a lot of time. And we're able to customize the fields that come back based on the clay table. So if we decide that reps need to see new information, that's all fully customizable here as well. And then if we go to salesforce, some of the things that we can do are things like buying committee enrichment. So here, if we go to Google and click on buying committee, what's going to happen is a really sophisticated play behind the scenes in clay. It will target the right personas for our specific ICP, dedupe against contacts we already have, surface the contacts we already have in salesforce, and then add the ones that aren't in salesforce. And then it will tell the reps exactly who to prospect next — based on our ICP, the people you should be reaching out to. It will link to their salesforce page so they can get more information about their marketing activity or sales activity, and then reach out to them. Normally this buying committee sourcing can take a lot of time, just standardizing and enabling teams. But this really helps us standardize that process. And again, pretty much anything you can do in clay, you can enable reps to surface that data and get that data in exportly, which is pretty cool. And then at a contact level, same thing: say a rep calls somebody and the phone number is bad — they can do a salesforce refresh to get a phone number, same with email address, and that will run through and populate the phone number both in this view for them and also in salesforce, so they can refresh data and information as needed. So this has really helped our team — honestly saved a ton of time. The match rates are pretty significant. I think we're seeing like 92% match rates compared to some other sources. And it has been a real game changer for our team.
