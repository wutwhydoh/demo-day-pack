> I'm good. Thanks, everyone. Good to see you. I am richard purcell. I currently work in marketing at a company called build. And build is construction finance. And so we sell to an industry that's very relationship based. And before I started using the swarm about three years ago, I had this macro problem where I was trying to think about how do you scale referrals — because referrals are the highest performing channel across verticals, but they're the least predictable. And so I was thinking about how do you make them more scalable. One of the things that you should be thinking about is not just who your linkedin connections are or who your investors know — investors have a very low ceiling for how many people they can introduce you to, and linkedin connections — no one knows their linkedin connections, and there's no context on who you're connected to. And so the way I think about relationship mapping is a 360 view of your network. How do you identify those relationships that are more repeatable? One example would be your champion accounts — your raving fans, where every single month you're having new people coming in and you could be mapping their relationships — but also thinking about membership organizations, alumni, vendors. And so it's like, okay, now how do we map these relationships? And you don't want to just ask anyone for an intro. You want to ask intros to companies that are already in market, and you want to look at all these different signals that could indicate intent, whether it's open job descriptions, public announcements. And so the thought is, how do you really do this in a scalable way and without having to switch tabs? And so what's great about pairing claude with the swarm is: with claude, you could have all these connectors such as your CRM, Google Drive, other connectors. And then with the swarm, you're able to really map relationships — a whole 360 degree view. The way this works is first you log into the swarm and you add all of your connectors — not just your employees, but content collaborators, loyal social media followers, really just a whole 360 degree view. And the swarm already starts to create context and maps the strength of those relationships for you. How I did this: first I connected the swarm to claude. But I first wanted to find which of my target accounts have signals that would indicate intent for our business. And so I put in a prompt like this — upload our account list as a CSV — and asked about tech stack innovation signals, customer overlap by integrating Salesforce. And so we got a list of our target accounts here prioritized by signals. But then I could go into claude again and use that same list and say, okay, now review this list and use the swarm MCP to map my entire network. And so then you get the output that looks like this. What we're looking at is all of our target accounts prioritized by overlapping signals and relationships. And as you see here, we can get in front of this person at one of our target accounts through this person. But what's really powerful is now you have the context of relationships. You're not going to want to ask an intro from someone who might have only worked with someone for a few months, like 10 years ago. But you also have other insights, because you've added other connectors — things like, oh, this person has a shared alumni [connection]. And to give you an example about the outcome of this: within 24 hours of passing this list to one of our SDRs, they booked a meeting with an EVP at a $2 billion company. So huge impact there.
