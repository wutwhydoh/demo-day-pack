> So when I first started at ask elephant, I got hired as the first sales hire. I was the seventh overall hire. My entire budget to run both marketing and top of funnel for outbound was $40 a month. And if you have done any level of outbound infrastructure, you know that there is not a dialer or a sequencer out there that you can use for 40 bucks a month. So what I did is I invested very heavily in linkedin. I got my founder on board. We built out a founder led brand that I capitalized on by doing linkedin outbound completely free. It was very scrappy and it worked incredibly well. We were doing 50, 60 meetings a month just off of that system. Now, the problem with it is whenever we tried to scale — and I had four or five BDRs trying to do that — everything fell apart. I couldn't see what the SDRs were sending. We weren't getting as many meetings as I knew we should be, but I couldn't know why. And that's where first touch really helped me. So with first touch, if I go over here to signals and I go over to configure, I can actually see everybody that's engaged on my content recently, or my content on [the founder's]. And I can actually throw those into a flow. It's going through a qualification agent to see what's ICP, and then creating tasks like sending connection requests and sending messages on linkedin that are measurable, have metrics on. And all of this is being drafted for me, so I can go do it manually or I can automate some of it. You want to be careful with the linkedin terms of service, of course — I'm not just going to be mass blasting this, but I'm sending it to the people who are already engaged. And beyond that, I can add in call and email tasks and other tasks that get pushed to hubspot and into our regular sequencer. So I can build a social first sequence. Now, doing this, we are actually seeing things like — if I go over here to metrics — we've gotten 28 opportunities off of this in the last seven days, just off of my account. If I go back to the dashboard, you can see we have a 98% reply rate — where is that — right here, I'm through 100%. Apparently I just got another message. On things that start with social. And when they don't reply, then we can move to a channel that they do prefer. And so that's first touch. It's been incredibly useful for us.
